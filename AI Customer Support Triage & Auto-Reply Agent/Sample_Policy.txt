Refund Policy: If an item arrives damaged, customers are eligible for 
a full refund or free replacement within 30 days of delivery. Customers 
should provide their order ID and a photo of the damaged item.

Shipping Policy: Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days. Express 
shipping takes 2-3 business days.

Cancellation Policy: Orders can be cancelled free of charge within 
24 hours of purchase. After that, cancellation is not guaranteed if 
the order has already shipped.
