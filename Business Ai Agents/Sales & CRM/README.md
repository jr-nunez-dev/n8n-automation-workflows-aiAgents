# 💼 Sales & CRM AI Agents

A collection of AI-powered sales and CRM agents built with n8n, focused on automating lead management, customer engagement, sales qualification, and CRM operations through intelligent workflow automation.

This folder serves as a growing library of sales and CRM AI agents designed to assist sales teams, improve lead quality, streamline customer interactions, and enhance sales productivity.

AI agents in this category may include:

- 📊 Lead scoring assistants

- 🤝 Customer engagement agents
