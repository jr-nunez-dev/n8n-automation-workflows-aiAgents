# 🤖 Business AI Agents

A collection of AI-powered business agents built with n8n, focused on solving real-world business challenges through intelligent automation, decision-making, and seamless system integrations.

This folder serves as a growing library of AI agents designed to enhance business operations across various domains by automating repetitive tasks, assisting users with data-driven decisions, and improving operational efficiency.

## What You'll Find

AI agents in this category may include:

- 🎧 Customer support assistants
- 💼 Sales and CRM agents
- 🛠️ IT operations assistants
- 📅 Productivity assistants
- 📊 Business intelligence agents
- 📈 Process optimization agents
- 📧 Communication assistants
- 🔄 Workflow automation agents

As the repository grows, additional business AI agents covering different industries and business functions will be added.
